# Open Questions — Resolve Before Implementation/Procurement

1. What tractor class/model or new chassis is in scope?
2. What is the first crop and first task: crop-row navigation, spraying, weeding, fertilizer or another implement?
3. What row spacing, implement width, wheelbase, turning radius, max slope and target speed apply?
4. What field sizes and soil/terrain conditions are expected?
5. What is the target BOM and development budget?
6. Is the first prototype simulation-only, bench hardware, or a retrofit tractor?
7. Which steering/brake/engine/implement interfaces are vendor-supported?
8. What RTK correction service is available in the intended operating region?
9. Which camera placement, lens and weather protection are feasible?
10. What dataset collection and annotation budget/team are available?
11. Which latency, detection, crop-damage and stop-response thresholds should be accepted?
12. What Android minimum version and rugged device are required?
13. What cloud provider/data residency/retention requirements apply?
14. Who is accountable for safety engineering and applicable standards?
15. Which approvals are required before a closed-field trial in India?

## 2. Procurement & Safety Gates

1. **Gate 1 — Platform Selection & Hydraulics Verification:** Confirm tractor hydraulic system type (Open Center vs. Closed Center Load Sensing - CCLS) to select compatible Danfoss steering valve manifold.
2. **Gate 2 — Third-Party Drive-by-Wire Authority:** Obtain manufacturer authorization or legal conversion sign-off for steering, brake, and throttle override.
3. **Gate 3 — Power Conditioning Validation:** Bench-test power supply against ISO 7637-2 transient generators before connecting Jetson compute.
4. **Gate 4 — Safety Controller & Wireless E-Stop Certification:** Inspect factory certification documents (AgPL d / Cat 3) for the wireless remote safety receiver and safety PLC.
5. **Gate 5 — Sensor Cleaning Verification:** Install and validate 6-bar pneumatic air-blast purge valve on camera and LiDAR lenses under dry harrowing dust conditions.

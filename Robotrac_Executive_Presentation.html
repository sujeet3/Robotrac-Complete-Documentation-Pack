⚠️ Why Passenger AV Stacks Fail in Agriculture
  • Terramechanics & Non-Zero Slip: Deformable tilled soil invalidates bicycle kinematic assumptions. Lateral slip (vy ≠ 0) causes steady-state downhill drift into crop rows.
  • Severe Environmental Blinding: Airborne soil dust and crop chaff blind optical cameras and LiDAR lenses within 20 minutes without active cleaning.
  • High-Mast Antenna Roll: Antenna mounted at 2.8 m height introduces up to 29.2 cm lateral error on a 6° furrow tilt if uncompensated.
🎯 Robotrac Engineering Objectives
  • Dual-Domain Compute: Hard Real-Time safety MCU for 100 Hz hydraulic steering, decoupled from Linux/Perception.
  • Sub-Scale Mule Validation: Eliminate sim-to-real hazard via an electric drive-by-wire mule before full-size diesel deployment.
  • Zero-Cloud Autonomous Independence: 100% of steering, obstacle stopping, and swath following executed onboard.

  • Failsafe Stopping: Spring-Applied Pressure-Released (SAPR) brake dump valve (< 150 ms).
  • Powertrain & Implement: SAE J1939 throttle (TSC1) + ISOBUS 3-point hitch lift at headlands.

Section 04 · State Estimation

Dual-Antenna RTK & 3D Tilt Compensation

Sub-2.5 cm Accuracy
📡 Moving Baseline Dual-Antenna RTK
  • Static Heading: Dual antennas on a ≥ 1.2 m baseline across cab roof.
  • Zero-Speed Accuracy: Calculates carrier-phase double-differences, yielding true static heading (σψ < 0.09°) even at 0 m/s forward velocity.
  • Immunity: Completely unaffected by tractor steel chassis magnetic disturbances or wheel spin in mud.
📐 Dynamic Terrain Roll / Pitch Compensation
  • Tactical IMU: Xsens MTi-680G (< 2°/hr bias stability) executing at 200 Hz.
  • Lever-Arm Correction: Dynamic coordinate transformation down to ground contact patch:
    Δx = h · sin(θ_roll) + l · cos(θ_pitch)
  • Ground Truth: Limits ground lateral tilt error to ≤ 2.0 cm over severe ruts and furrows.

Section 07 · Coverage Path Planning

Fields2Cover Swaths & Ω-Turns

Implement Footprint Aware
🗺️ Agricultural Coverage Hierarchy
  • Headland Generator: Computes peripheral buffer zones based on tractor wheelbase and implement turning radius Rmin.
  • Swath Generator: Computes optimal parallel swaths aligned with field contours to minimize soil compaction and overlap.
  • Route Optimizer: Solves swath traversal order via Google OR-Tools to minimize non-working headland travel time.
🔄 Non-Holonomic Turn Trajectories
  • Ω-Turn (Omega Turn): Loops outside the swath when adjacent tracks are closer than 2 · Rmin.
  • Dubins & Reeds-Shepp: Generates kinematically feasible forward/reverse turning curves.
  • Swept Collision Envelope: Simulates towed implement swing moments to avoid hitting fence posts and field ditches.

⚡ The Control Formulation
  • Receding Horizon: Prediction horizon N = 20 steps (Ts = 0.05 s, lookahead = 1.0 s).
  • Soil-Tire Slip Dynamics: Incorporates Pacejka lateral tire-soil friction models (Fyf, Fyr) directly into state equations.
  • Implement Drag Observer: Compensates for dynamic yaw perturbations caused by asymmetric tillage soil resistance.
🔒 Hard Actuation Constraints
  • Knuckle Angle Bounds: |δsteer| ≤ 35°

  • Tip-Over Prevention: Lateral acceleration capped at |ay| ≤ 0.25 · g on side-slopes.
  • Solve Latency: Real-Time Iterations (RTI) converge in ≤ 20 ms on Jetson Orin.
